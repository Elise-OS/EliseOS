Élise OS – Companion is the iPhone app for family members and caregivers looking after someone who lives alone at home. It connects to the Élise OS server installed in their home and lets you know when something is out of the ordinary, without constant monitoring.

Élise relies on discreet sensors (motion, presence, door opening, bed, temperature, smoke, water leak, fall…) and learns the home’s routines. When an unusual situation comes up, you are notified.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• See at a glance whether the day is going as usual at home.
• Get a notification when a fall is detected, or in case of smoke, a water leak, carbon monoxide, a press of the assistance button or prolonged inactivity.
• Choose which notifications you want to receive.
• Follow an alert, take it in hand and see the readings that triggered it.
• Keep in touch with the family and care workers through the shared notebook: messages, moods, tasks and visits.
• Check the shared calendar: appointments, visits and reminders.
• Video call the person on their TV and send them photos and messages.
• View the home’s cameras live, only if they were installed with the person’s consent.
• Install and place sensors room by room using the home floor plan.

YOUR DATA STAYS AT HOME
Élise OS runs locally: sensor data and routine analysis stay on the server installed in the home, not in a manufacturer’s cloud. When you are away, the app connects through a private, encrypted access. Cameras are optional, can be switched to private mode at any time, and their images are never used to analyse behaviour.

FREE SOFTWARE
Élise OS is free and open source software (AGPL-3.0 licence).

REQUIREMENTS
This app requires an Élise OS server installed in the home (Raspberry Pi or Ubuntu server) and an account created by that server’s administrator. It does not work on its own.

IMPORTANT
Élise OS is a wellbeing and peace-of-mind tool. It is not a medical device, a personal alarm service or an emergency service. In an emergency, call your local emergency number (112 in Europe, 911 in the US).
